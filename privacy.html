ThunderBars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ThunderBars ("the App") is a music visualizer for iOS. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your data safe.

Data collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not require an account and does not contain ads, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Microphone

The App uses your device's microphone to analyze audio in real time and render visualizations. All audio processing happens locally on your device. Audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.

Lyrics

If you enable the synced lyrics feature, the App may query a lyrics service to display song lyrics while music plays. Only the information needed to look up lyrics for the currently playing song is sent, and it is not stored by us.

Third-party services

The App may connect to third-party services (for example, a lyrics provider) solely to deliver the features you request. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Beta program

If you join the beta program via the sign-up form, we collect the contact information you voluntarily provide (such as your email address) solely to invite you to the beta. This data is processed by our form provider (Formspree) under its own privacy policy and is not sold or shared.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
